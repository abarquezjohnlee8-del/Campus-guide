Eating Well on a Student Budget

Your first year is busy: new classes, new friends, and long days on campus It is easy to skip meals or spend your whole allowance on milk tea. Eating regular, simple meals helps you focus in class, and you do not need a big budget to do it.

Where to Eat Near Campus

You have more options than you think.Here are the usual spots:

  • The school canteen for quick rice meals between classes.
  • Carinderias outside the gate for cheap,filling ulam and rice.
  • Convenience stores for emergency snacks, not for every meals.

How to Plan Your Weekly Food Budget

A little planning on Sunday saves you from being broke on Thursday.

  1. Check your allowance you have for the whole week.
  2. Set aside money for fare and school needs first

  3. Divide what is left by tge number of school days.
  4. Pack baon on at least two days to stretch your budget.
  5. Keep a small amount for emergencies, like a surprise project.

Quick Tips

Always bring a refillable water bottle.Water stations on campus are free, and you will save a lot compared to buying bottled drinks every day.
